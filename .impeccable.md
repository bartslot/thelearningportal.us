## Design Context

### Users
K–12 teachers creating story-driven history lessons, often between classes or while planning. They need to turn a learning goal into a usable lesson quickly, without configuring an unfamiliar production pipeline.

### Brand Personality
Confident, cinematic, and teacher-centric. The interface should create trust and a sense of possibility while staying calm, direct, and professional rather than childish, corporate, or overexcited.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark navy surfaces with warm amber actions and restrained sky accents, using the existing Learning Portal DaisyUI theme and History display type. Teacher workflows should feel focused and editorial, with conversational pacing and subtle motion that explains state changes.

### Design Principles
- Make the next teacher action unmistakable and explain why it matters.
- Reveal one decision at a time instead of presenting a configuration panel.
- Use concise, natural language that sounds like a capable teaching partner.
- Preserve teacher control: suggestions remain easy to accept or replace.
- Keep motion purposeful, accessible, and subordinate to task speed.
